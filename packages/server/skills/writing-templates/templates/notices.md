# 通知 / 申请 / 致谢 / 道歉

## 通知公告
标题：关于<事项>的通知
正文：为<目的>，现将<事项>通知如下：一、时间…；二、范围/对象…；三、要求…。如有疑问请联系<人/电话>。<部门>，<日期>

## 请示 / 申请
标题：关于<事项>的请示
正文：<背景与原因，附数据>。为此，申请<具体事项、金额、时间>。妥否，请批示。附件：…

## 致谢信（客户）
Dear <Name>, Thank you for <specific thing: your order No. …/visit to our factory>. <One sentence on what it means / next step>. We look forward to <next milestone>. Best regards,

## 道歉信（延期/失误）
Dear <Name>, I'm sorry that <what happened, take responsibility>. This was caused by <brief reason, no excuses>. To make it right, we will <concrete remedy + date>. We have also <preventive action>. Thank you for your patience. Best regards,
